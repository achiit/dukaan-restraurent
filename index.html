From the owner

Hello, I am Ram

My father used to say a meal tastes better when the whole family is at one table. When we started this restaurant in Noida, I wanted a place where my parents, my cousins and their small children could all find something they like.

So we put everything under one roof: proper Indian food, fast food for the kids, a cafe for your evening chai, and a play zone where children can run around while you finish your meal. We are new, and we are working hard to earn your next visit.

  • Open from 7 AM to 11 PM, every day
  • Separate children's play zone
  • Clean, hygienic kitchen and dining hall
  • Comfortable seating for families and groups
  • Indian, fast food and cafe menus together
